Welcome to Promote Humanity Drive ("P.H.D.", "we", "us", or "our").

Promote Humanity Drive is a humanitarian and welfare management application designed to assist authorized users and organizations in managing information related to humanitarian activities, donors, cases, and related operational records.

This Privacy Policy explains how Promote Humanity Drive collects, uses, stores, protects, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and Authentication Information

If you are provided with or create an application account, we may process:

  • Username
  • Authentication credentials
  • Account identifier
  • User role
  • Account status
  • Account creation information
  • Information necessary to manage access to the application

This information is used to authenticate users and control access to features based on their assigned role.

1.2 Donor Information

Authorized users may enter donor-related information, which may include:

  • Donor name
  • Phone number
  • Donation amount
  • Notes or other information entered by an authorized user
  • Record creation and update dates

This information is used to maintain donation and donor records for humanitarian and organizational purposes.

1.3 Case Information

Authorized users may enter information relating to humanitarian or welfare cases, including:

  • Case identifier
  • Case description
  • Case status
  • Relevant dates
  • Images
  • Videos
  • Other information entered by authorized users for case management

1.4 Images and Videos

The application may allow authorized users to upload images or videos associated with cases or humanitarian activities.

Users should only upload media that they are authorized to provide and that does not unlawfully disclose another person's private information.

1.5 Device and Technical Information

The application and its third-party services may process technical information such as:

  • Device or app information
  • IP address
  • Advertising-related identifiers
  • App version
  • Operating system information
  • Device model
  • Diagnostic information
  • Crash or performance information
  • Information relating to advertising interactions

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Authenticate users
  • Provide authorized application features
  • Manage user roles
  • Store and retrieve humanitarian records
  • Manage donor records
  • Manage case records
  • Store authorized images and videos
  • Maintain application security
  • Detect unauthorized access or abuse
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Improve application performance
  • Display advertisements where applicable

3. Supabase and Data Storage

Promote Humanity Drive uses Supabase for backend services, authentication, database storage, and related application functionality.

Information submitted through the application may be transmitted to and stored using these services.

This may include account information, donor records, case records, images, videos, and related application data.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Google AdMob

Promote Humanity Drive may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob and related technologies may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location information, and information relating to advertising interactions, depending on applicable settings, region, and consent.

Advertising practices are subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and controls.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application, including:

  • Supabase — backend, authentication, and database services
  • Google AdMob — advertising services

  • Other service providers where necessary to operate the application

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, governmental request, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of users, our organization, or others.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Unauthorized disclosure
  • Unauthorized modification
  • Loss
  • Misuse
  • Destruction

Where appropriate, information is transmitted using encrypted connections. Access to application information is restricted according to user roles and operational requirements.

However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for:

  • Providing application functionality

  • Meeting legal obligations

  • Preventing fraud or abuse
